How it works

  1. Add her username here.

    Add her name and nicknames too. Those catch posts that talk about her without tagging her.

  2. Make the list of accounts to check.

    On her profile, tap Following and screenshot the list as you scroll. Add the screenshots on the Accounts tab and the page writes down every username.

  3. Go through each account and take screenshots.

    Open the comments under each post, the account's Tagged tab and its Reels. Upload the screenshots on the Scan tab, as many at once as you like.

  4. Look over what turned up.

    Every screenshot that shows her lands in Findings with what was found. Mark each account "Nothing found" once you're done with it.

What it can't do

  • It can't open Instagram or go through accounts for you. Instagram doesn't allow that, so you take the screenshots and this page does the reading.
  • It only sees what's in your screenshots. Accounts that are private to you, and comments she deleted, won't show up.
  • It reads text. It can't recognize her face. If you see a photo of her with no name or tag, keep it with "Save anyway".
  • "Possible" means something looks close to her username or matches her name. Check those before you rely on them.

If you find something worrying

  • Keep a copy before it gets deleted. This page saves each finding with the date.
  • Don't argue with the other account. Report it to Instagram: the ⋯ menu on their profile, then Report.
  • If an adult is asking her for photos, money or to meet up, or she's in danger, call the police. In an emergency, call 911.
  • If it shows sexual images of anyone under 18, don't save or share them, not even to show someone. Report them to the NCMEC CyberTipline at report.cybertip.org or 1-800-843-5678.

Accounts to check

    Scan screenshots

    Upload screenshots from the accounts on your list: comments, tagged posts, reels, stories. Each one that shows her is saved to Findings.

    Leave it blank and the account is worked out from each screenshot.

      Where to look on each account
      • Under each post, open all the comments and scroll. Replies are hidden until you tap "View replies", so open those too.
      • The account's Tagged tab (the person icon on their profile) shows posts people were tagged in.
      • Reels have their own comments. Tap the speech bubble.
      • Story highlights (the circles under the bio) sometimes show screenshots of messages or tags.
      • If you can open the list of likes on a post, screenshot that too.
